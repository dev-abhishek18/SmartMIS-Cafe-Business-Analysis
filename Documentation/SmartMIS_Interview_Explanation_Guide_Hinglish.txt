SMARTMIS – CAFE BUSINESS PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS
INTERVIEW EXPLANATION GUIDE (HINGLISH)

1. PROJECT INTRODUCTION
“SmartMIS ek Excel-based Management Information System project hai. Iska purpose café ke transaction data ko organize karke owner ko sales, store, product, category aur time-period performance samajhne mein help karna hai.”

Important: Demo transactions, sample customer details, product mix and operating costs are synthetic. Interview mein ise clearly demo/portfolio project bolna hai.

2. BUSINESS PROBLEM
Owner ke paas transaction records hone ke baad bhi kuch practical sawalon ka jawab nikalna mushkil ho sakta hai:
- Total sales aur orders kitne hain?
- Kaunsa store best perform kar raha hai?
- Kaunse products zyada/kaam revenue contribute karte hain?
- Month-wise sales aur gross margin ka trend kya hai?
- Weekdays ke comparison mein weekends ki average daily sales kaisi hai?

4. KPI DEFINITIONS
Total Sales = selected transactions ke sales amounts ka sum.
Orders = workbook ke order/transaction definition ke hisaab se count.
Units Sold = total quantity.
AOV = Total Sales / Orders.
COGS = sample product cost assumptions ke mutabik cost of goods sold.
Gross Profit = Sales - COGS.
Gross Margin % = Gross Profit / Sales × 100.

Gross profit aur margin sample cost assumptions par depend karte hain. Inhe audited financial result nahi batana.

7. COMMON INTERVIEW QUESTIONS
Q: Is project ka business objective kya hai?
A: Café owner ko sales, product, store aur time-period performance samajhne ke liye ek consolidated MIS view dena.

Q: AOV kya hota hai?
A: Average Order Value. Total sales ko total orders se divide karte hain.

Q: Gross profit aur net profit mein kya difference hai?
A: Gross profit sales minus COGS hai. Net profit calculate karne ke liye rent, salaries, utilities, marketing, tax aur doosre expenses bhi account karne hote hain. Is demo ko audited net profit report nahi kehna.

Q: Weekend sales kaise compare ki?
A: Total sales ke saath average daily sales bhi compare ki, kyunki weekdays aur weekend days ki count alag hoti hai.

Q: Inventory analysis kyon nahi hai?
A: Reliable opening stock, purchases, wastage aur closing stock details available nahi the. Isliye unsupported inventory calculations add nahi ki.

Q: Kya data real hai?
A: Nahi. Portfolio demonstration ke liye synthetic transaction and sample cost data use hua hai.

Q: Report ka period kya hai?
A: January to June 2026. Ye first-half analysis hai, complete 2026 annual report nahi.
